?How this works
  1. While he stays on a page, the brain model's reward and attention response is measured.
  2. It is compared with what his preferences for that page's topics predicted; the difference nudges those topics up or down.
  3. Old preferences fade over about a week, so topics he stops visiting slowly drop out.

These numbers come from the simulated responses of his brain model to the pages he opened.
