## Grip notes

- **ref1**: Seated in a wicker chair, seen over the shoulder, with a paperback held open in both hands and tilted up toward the face. Each hand cradles a cover from below and outside. The left palm is behind the left cover, with the fingertips curling over the left page edge. The right palm and fingers are under the back cover, with the thumb pressing the lower right page margin.
- **ref2**: Seated in a wingback armchair by a fireplace, with a hardback held upright and open on the lap at chest height. The left hand supports the left cover from below, with the thumb on the inner bottom margin of the left page. The right hand grips the lower outer corner of the right half, with the thumb and fingertips on the bottom margin and the palm behind the cover.
- **ref3** (page turn, seated, POV): Seated cross-legged on grass, looking down at an old hardback. The far hand holds the book from underneath, by the spine and back cover. The near hand's index and middle fingertips lift and flip the lower right page corner. This is a fingertip flick, not a pinch.
- **ref4** (page-turn close-up, bonus): Macro shot of a page turn using a thumb-and-finger pinch. The thumb lies flat on top of a bunch of page edges at the outer margin, with the index and middle fingers curled underneath. The pages lift in a curl away from the text block.
