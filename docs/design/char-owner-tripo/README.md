# The owner's own Tripo character (2026-10-07)

Generated by the owner in Tripo Studio, in one piece, from the four body views in `../char-tripo/refs/` (the H3.1
model, Ultra mesh, AI Complete, 4K PBR texture with lighting removed, triangles). AI-generated content under Tripo's
terms; an original fictional ~20-year-old man, not a real person, no brands or logos.

- `char_owner_tripo.glb`: as he downloaded it. One mesh, 482,690 triangles, 252,066 vertices, no skin, 4096²
  textures (base colour, normal, metal/roughness). 0.98 units tall, facing +Z, A-pose.
- `sheet.jpg`: four sides, face, the collar from the front, back and above, and the feet from four sides.

Why it exists: v5's head is a separate Tripo bust grafted onto the body, and the graft left the gap at the collar
the owner kept pointing at. In one piece, the neck goes into the hood with no seam. It is the candidate to replace v5,
rigged by Mixamo's auto-rigger (Standard Skeleton, 65 joints with fingers) so that the Mixamo captures play on their
own skeleton.

The Mixamo upload is made with `room_build/rig/normalize.mjs` (1.75 m, feet on y = 0), `simplify.mjs` (0.33, 159,286
triangles) and `to_obj.mjs`.
