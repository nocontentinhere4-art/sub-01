# The Tripo props

Four props made with Tripo (paid API) take the place of earlier ones in the room. The details and task ids are in
`web/room/CREDITS.md`, "The Tripo props".

- `props.webp`: the four props, rendered alone (ramen bowl, mug, headphones, sneakers).
- `tripo_props_preview.mp4`: the room before (left, `?props=old`) and after (right), side by side, 30 fps, frame-stepped
  with the visual QA harness. It shows the overview, the mug on the desk, the headphones on the hook, the sneakers, then
  him eating the ramen. Made by `python room_build/tripo_preview.py`.

Known: the ramen bowl comes with its own chopsticks resting across the rim (the brief asked for them), so while he eats
there are two pairs, his and the bowl's. When he finishes, the empty bowl is still Kenney's `bowl`. His left hand
reaching into the dish is the page's eating pose and happens with the earlier dishes too.
